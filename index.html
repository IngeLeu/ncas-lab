Our approach

Mechanisms first. Stimulation follows.

Our projects span different brain systems, populations and stimulation techniques, but share one principle: effective intervention starts with understanding the circuit and the neural state we want to influence.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Neural circuit mechanisms

We investigate oscillations, transient brain states and cortico-subcortical interactions that shape movement, inhibition, memory and cognition.

02 · REACH

Non-invasive circuit stimulation

We use and develop stimulation approaches that can influence distributed and deeper brain circuits, including tACS and transcranial ultrasound.

03 · ADAPT

Closed-loop stimulation

We combine mechanistic knowledge with real-time neural read-outs so stimulation can be timed and tailored to the ongoing state of the brain.

Current directions

Different circuits, one experimental logic.

We do not define the lab by one oscillation, one region or one disorder. We use circuit mechanisms to decide what to measure, where to intervene and when stimulation should be delivered.
