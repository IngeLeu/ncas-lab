Join us

Work on questions that cross methods and disciplines.

We are interested in students and collaborators who want to understand neural circuits and develop better ways to interact with them.

Students

Projects can be suitable for Bachelor, Master and Research Master students, depending on the question and available supervision. Relevant interests include EEG and signal analysis, non-invasive brain stimulation, motor control, memory, Parkinson’s disease, ageing and closed-loop methods.

PhD and postdoctoral researchers

Funded positions will be advertised when available. We also welcome conversations with researchers interested in developing fellowship proposals that fit the lab’s focus on neural circuits and adaptive stimulation.

Collaborations

We work closely with clinical, computational and engineering partners. If you have complementary expertise in real-time decoding, neurotechnology, neurology, neurosurgery, ageing or neurodegenerative disease, we are happy to discuss possible connections.
