People

Different expertise, shared questions.

NCAS is built around team science. We combine cognitive neuroscience, clinical neuroscience, multimodal imaging, electrophysiology and brain stimulation to understand neural circuits and develop more adaptive ways of interacting with them.

Current team

Researchers

IL

Inge Leunissen

Assistant Professor · group lead

Inge studies neural circuit mechanisms that support flexible behaviour and develops non-invasive, adaptive stimulation approaches to target those mechanisms. Her work combines EEG, TMS, tACS, neuroimaging and real-time neural decoding, with current translational projects in Parkinson’s disease, ageing and Alzheimer’s disease.

GD

Gopika Dharmendra

External PhD researcher

Gopika investigates beta–gamma coupling and its causal role in motor inhibition. Her work examines how tACS can influence cross-frequency coupling, whether stimulation shifts individual peak frequencies, and how stimulation effects differ between oscillatory and non-oscillatory components of neural activity.

RV

Rick Voncken

PhD researcher · ALZ-LOOP

Rick studies closed-loop interventions for memory improvement across translational models. His project bridges animal and human work to test how temporally precise stimulation of memory-related neural dynamics can improve memory and inform future interventions for ageing and Alzheimer’s disease.

DZ

Dominik Zeman

PhD researcher · TRIPSIM

Dominik investigates body–brain interactions using a multimodal combination of fMRI, EEG and TMS. By bringing together simultaneous and complementary measures of brain activity with causal stimulation, the project aims to understand how bodily and neural processes interact to shape cognition and behaviour.

OM

Oscar Magnusson

PhD researcher

Oscar studies anticipatory synchronization in neural communication, examining how temporal relationships between signals may support efficient information transfer across brain networks.
